# Martech Stack Templates by Company Stage

## Stage 1: Pre-PMF / Seed (< $500k ARR, < 5 people in marketing)

**Goal:** Collect data, test channels, iterate fast. Minimize cost and complexity.

| Layer | Tool | Cost |
|-------|------|------|
| Foundation | Google Tag Manager | Free |
| Foundation | Consent Management | Cookiebot (~$10/mo) |
| Collection | GA4 | Free |
| Collection | Hotjar or Clarity | Free |
| Orchestration | HubSpot Starter | $50/mo |
| Activation | Mailchimp or HubSpot email | Included |
| Activation | Google Ads / Meta Ads | Variable |

**Total stack cost:** ~$60–100/month + ad spend

---

## Stage 2: Early Growth ($500k–$5M ARR, 2–5 person marketing team)

**Goal:** Scale what works. Add behavioral tracking and attribution.

| Layer | Tool | Cost |
|-------|------|------|
| Foundation | GTM + Consent | ~$50/mo |
| Collection | GA4 + Mixpanel or PostHog | $0–$250/mo |
| Collection | Session recording (Hotjar) | ~$40/mo |
| Data Hub | Segment (Team) or RudderStack | ~$120/mo |
| Orchestration | HubSpot Pro | ~$800/mo |
| Orchestration | Customer.io or Klaviyo | $100–300/mo |
| Enrichment | Clearbit or Apollo | $200–500/mo |
| Activation | Ad platforms + Intercom | Variable |

**Total stack cost:** ~$1,500–2,500/month + ad spend

---

## Stage 3: Scaling ($5M–$20M ARR, 5–15 person marketing team)

**Goal:** Full-funnel attribution, account-based marketing, expansion revenue.

| Layer | Tool | Cost |
|-------|------|------|
| Foundation | GTM Enterprise or Tealium | ~$500/mo |
| Collection | GA4 + Amplitude | ~$500/mo |
| Data Hub | Segment Business + Snowflake or BigQuery | ~$2,000/mo |
| Data Hub | Census or Hightouch (reverse ETL) | ~$1,000/mo |
| Orchestration | HubSpot Enterprise or Salesforce | $1,500–3,000/mo |
| Orchestration | Marketo or Customer.io | ~$1,000/mo |
| Enrichment | Clearbit + ZoomInfo or Clay | ~$2,000/mo |
| Attribution | Rockerbox or Triple Whale | ~$500/mo |
| Activation | Intercom, Braze or Iterable | ~$2,000/mo |

**Total stack cost:** ~$10,000–15,000/month + ad spend

---

## Stage 4: Enterprise ($20M+ ARR, 15+ person marketing team)

**Goal:** Personalization at scale, ABM, sophisticated attribution, compliance.

| Layer | Tool |
|-------|------|
| Foundation | Tealium iQ or Ensighten |
| Collection | Adobe Analytics or GA4 360 |
| Data Hub | Snowflake + Fivetran + full CDP (Segment, mParticle, or Tealium AudienceStream) |
| Orchestration | Salesforce + Marketo or Pardot |
| ABM | Demandbase, 6sense, or Terminus |
| Attribution | Rockerbox, Northbeam, or custom |
| Activation | Braze, Iterable, or Adobe Campaign |
| Enrichment | ZoomInfo Enterprise + Clay |
