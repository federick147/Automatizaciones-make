# North Star Metric Playbook

## NSM Selection Criteria

A good North Star Metric:
1. **Reflects customer value** — not internal activity (not "emails sent," but "replies received")
2. **Predictive** — correlates with long-term retention and revenue
3. **Actionable** — teams can influence it with product and marketing changes
4. **Single metric** — not a composite; forces prioritization
5. **Understandable** — anyone on the team can explain it

## NSM by Business Model

| Model | Candidate NSM | Why |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| B2B SaaS (seats) | Weekly active accounts | Predicts renewal and expansion |
| B2B SaaS (usage-based) | Core actions per account per week | Drives expansion revenue |
| Consumer social | DAU / WAU (stickiness) | Predicts ad revenue and retention |
| Marketplace | Successful transactions per week | Both sides deliver value |
| E-commerce | Repeat purchase rate (90-day) | LTV driver |
| Media / content | Articles / videos consumed per user per week | Predicts ad revenue |
| Productivity tool | Core task completed per active user | Stickiness and habit formation |
| Gaming | D7 retention or sessions per week | Engagement predictor |
| Fintech | Transactions per active user | Revenue and engagement |

## Input Metrics (the levers)

Map 3–5 input metrics to each NSM. These are the leading indicators.

### Framework:
- **Breadth**: % of users who do the core action at all
- **Depth**: How much of the core action they do
- **Frequency**: How often they do it
- **Efficiency**: How quickly they reach value (Time to Value)

### Example: B2B SaaS NSM = "Weekly Active Accounts"

| Input Metric | Type | Target |
|-------------|------|--------|
| % of accounts active in last 7 days | Breadth | > 60% |
| Core actions per active account per week | Depth | > 5 |
| % of new accounts active by Day 7 | Efficiency | > 40% |
| % of accounts active last week, active this week | Frequency | > 70% |

## Common NSM Mistakes

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Choosing a revenue metric | MRR | MRR is output, not value delivered |
| Vanity metric | Signups, page views | These don't correlate with retention |
| Composite metric | "Engagement score" | Too hard to act on; break into inputs |
| Output of output | Revenue per employee | Too indirect |
| Gaming risk | Emails sent | Teams will spam users to hit the number |

## NSM Review Cadence

- **Weekly**: Review input metrics on team dashboard
- **Monthly**: Review NSM trend and cohort health
- **Quarterly**: Reassess whether NSM still best captures value
