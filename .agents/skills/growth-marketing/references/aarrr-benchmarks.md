# AARRR Benchmarks by Industry and Stage

## Acquisition

| Channel | Typical CAC Range | Payback Period |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| Organic/SEO | $10–$50 | < 3 months |
| Content marketing | $20–$100 | 3–6 months |
| Paid social (B2C) | $5–$50 | 1–3 months |
| Paid search (B2B SaaS) | $200–$800 | 6–18 months |
| Outbound sales | $500–$2,000 | 12–24 months |
| Referral/viral | $1–$20 | < 1 month |

## Activation

| Product Type | Good Activation Rate | Definition of Activation |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| B2B SaaS | > 40% | Completes core setup action |
| Consumer app | > 30% | Returns within 7 days of signup |
| E-commerce | > 25% | Completes first purchase |
| Marketplace (supply) | > 20% | Creates first listing |
| Marketplace (demand) | > 35% | Completes first transaction |

## Retention (Day 30)

| Product Type | Excellent | Good | Needs Work |
|-------------|-----------|------|------------|
| B2B SaaS (monthly churn) | < 1% | 1–2% | > 3% |
| Consumer social | > 40% | 25–40% | < 20% |
| Mobile game | > 10% | 5–10% | < 5% |
| E-commerce (90-day repeat) | > 45% | 25–45% | < 20% |
| News/media (DAU/MAU) | > 50% | 25–50% | < 20% |

## Referral (Viral Coefficient — K-factor)

| K-factor | Growth Type |
|----------|-------------|
| > 1.0 | Viral — exponential growth without other channels |
| 0.5–1.0 | Strong supplement to paid/organic acquisition |
| 0.2–0.5 | Meaningful contribution |
| < 0.2 | Minimal viral contribution |

## Revenue

| Metric | B2B SaaS Target | Consumer Target |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| LTV:CAC | > 3:1 | > 2:1 |
| CAC Payback | < 12 months | < 6 months |
| Net Revenue Retention | > 110% | > 100% |
| Gross margin | > 70% | > 50% |

## Quick Ratio by Stage

| Stage | Quick Ratio Target |
|-------|------------------|
| Pre-PMF | N/A — focus on qualitative retention |
| Early growth | > 2 |
| Scaling | > 4 |
| Mature | > 2 (harder to maintain at scale) |
